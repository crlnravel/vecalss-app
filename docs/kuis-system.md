# How the Kuis system works today

This guide covers the Kuis area used by Kapten and students. A quiz attached directly to a Materi lesson and a Tryout are separate flows.

## 1. Kapten prepares a Kuis

Kapten creates a Kuis for a subject, adds questions from Bank Soal, and sets its rules. These can include when the Kuis opens and closes, its time limit, how many tries a student gets, the wait before trying again, and when scores or correct answers can be seen.

Only Kuis that are available to students appear in the student list. Once a student starts, that attempt keeps the questions and rules it began with, even if Kapten edits the Kuis later.

## 2. A student takes it

The student opens the Kuis list, chooses a subject and Kuis, then starts an attempt. The system checks that the Kuis is open and that another try is allowed. If the student already has an unfinished attempt, they can continue it instead of starting over.

Answers are saved as the student works. Questions can ask for one answer or several. When a timed attempt reaches its deadline, the system submits it using the answers saved so far. The student can also submit before time runs out.

## 3. Scores and review

After submission, the system checks the answers and records the score. Kapten can review student attempts. Students see their score, explanations, and correct answers according to the Kuis settings; these may be available right away, after the Kuis closes, or not shown.

If students get more than one try, Kapten chooses whether the first, latest, highest, or average score counts.

**In short:** Kapten prepares and schedules the Kuis; students take it and their answers are saved; the system scores it and shows results according to Kapten’s settings.
