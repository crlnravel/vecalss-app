# Copywriting & Indonesian Text Policy

**Purpose:** Ensure all student-facing copy, landing page content, UI microcopy, marketing text, and Indonesian prose sound authentic, natural, and human, free from robotic AI clichés and awkward translations.

## Core Rules

1. **Mandatory Skill for Copywriting:**
   Whenever dealing with any copywriting, UI copy, student/user-facing text, Indonesian prose, or content refinement task, agents **MUST** use and apply the `tutur-humanizer` skill:
   - Skill documentation: `.agents/skills/tutur-humanizer/SKILL.md`
   - Register guide: `.agents/skills/tutur-humanizer/references/register-map.md`
   - AI-cliché pattern bank: `.agents/skills/tutur-humanizer/references/pattern-bank.md`
   - Examples & comparisons: `.agents/skills/tutur-humanizer/references/examples.md`

2. **Style & Register Guidelines:**
   - **Default Voice:** Clear, natural Indonesian (mostly baku-santai: readable, polite, not bureaucratic, not slang-heavy).
   - **Avoid AI Tells:** Strip formulaic openers ("Di era digital yang terus berkembang...", "Dalam lanskap..."), empty buzzwords ("solusi inovatif", "komprehensif", "menjadi kunci", "mendorong transformasi"), and English calques ("menavigasi tantangan", "menghadirkan nilai").
   - **Tone Consistency:** Align register with the context:
     - Student & Voyager surfaces: Warm, encouraging, clear, natural Indonesian.
     - Kapten / Mentor workspace: Professional, efficient, respectful.
     - Admin & Operations: Direct, unambiguous, standard Indonesian.
     - Landing / Public pages: Engaging, benefit-driven, concise, zero corporate fluff.

3. **Truth & Integrity:**
   Never invent claims, features, testimonials, citations, statistics, or proof when humanizing text.
